About

Why declarations

A declaration is a word spoken with authority, in agreement with what has already been decided in heaven. It is not a request. It is a standing.

Scripture treats speech as a form of action. What is spoken is either building or tearing down, binding or loosing, agreeing with truth or agreeing with a lie. Declarations exist because words are not neutral.

This is why we do not mumble. We say it plainly, out loud, and we say it again tomorrow.
